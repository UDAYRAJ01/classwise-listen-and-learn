## Government exam pipeline
- This separate domain supports recruitment bodies, stages, sections, states and descriptive formats without touching JEE/NEET.
- A paper is ready only after all expected question numbers are present and its transcription passes a second image review; partial extraction is never used for frequency.
- Keep source provenance and key provenance distinct. Memory-based and model papers never count as real-PYQ evidence.
- Reruns upsert paper/question identity and preserve checked solutions only when question/options/answer are unchanged.
- Topic frequency is counted from reviewed stored questions, partitioned by exam, jurisdiction, stage and section. Future patterns are possibilities, not predictions; book references require stored source confirmation.
- Download and OCR scratch files live under /tmp/government; raw papers are linked to their official source, not republished as app assets.
